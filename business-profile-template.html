About

Hospitality as an Act of Worship

Solomon's Table has been serving weddings, church events and corporate gatherings across London for over eight years. Every menu is built around generous, excellent hospitality — the kind that makes people feel genuinely welcomed, not just fed.

What started as a husband-and-wife team cooking for their own church's Sunday gatherings has grown into a full events company — without ever losing that original heart for hospitality as ministry, not just a menu.

"We don't just cater events. We try to make sure everyone at that table feels seen, fed and welcomed — the way we believe God welcomes us."

Founded on the belief that how you serve a table says something about the God you serve.
