Verification Covers

A Minimum Standard You Can Trust

Every applicant is reviewed against eight areas, drawn from our verification questionnaire. A human reviewer always makes the final decision — no automated system approves or rejects an application.

  • ✓ Your business at a glance — the problem you solve, your offer, your customers and reach.
  • ✓ Trust & track record — credentials, case studies, testimonials and media.
  • ✓ Kingdom commitment — Christian ownership, your platform for soul-winning and biblical values in practice.
  • ✓ Legal & compliance — registration, licenses and statutory obligations.
  • ✓ Financial stewardship — accurate records, accounting systems, financial integrity.
  • ✓ Employment & culture — how you treat and lead your team.
  • ✓ Insurance & risk — cover in place and how risk is managed.
  • ✓ Customer experience — purchasing route, capacity, response time and feedback that reflect Christ.
