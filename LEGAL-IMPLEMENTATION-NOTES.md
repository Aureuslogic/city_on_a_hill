## Information still required

Before commercial launch, confirm and insert:

- Full legal entity name.
- Company or charity number, where applicable.
- Registered and correspondence address.
- Country of establishment.
- VAT or tax details, where applicable.
- Data-controller identity and privacy contact.
- Payment provider and merchant of record.
- Actual products, subscriptions, listing fees and event terms.
- Cancellation windows and service-start rules.
- Refund method and expected processing times.
- Content moderation, complaints and appeal process.
- Verification methodology and disclaimer.
- Retention periods by data category.
- Named processors, analytics providers and hosting services.
- International-transfer mechanisms.
- Cookie and consent-management approach.

## Data protection implementation

The community's religious identity means some information may reveal religious or philosophical beliefs, which can be special-category personal data. The final design must minimise collection, identify the lawful basis and additional condition where required, document consent where used, and restrict access appropriately.

## Refund and payment implementation

The refund page must be aligned with what is actually sold. Before enabling payments:

- Define whether the buyer is a consumer or business.
- Distinguish listings, memberships, digital content, services and event tickets.
- State when performance begins and how cancellation rights are affected.
- Record any required express request or acknowledgement.
- Explain event cancellation, postponement and transfer rules.
- Preserve statutory rights and avoid absolute “no refunds” wording.
- Ensure checkout wording, confirmation email and policy copy agree.

## Terms implementation

The final terms should match the live operating model, including:

- Platform role versus provider responsibility.
- Accuracy and ownership of submitted listing content.
- Outbound links and third-party transactions.
- Acceptable use and prohibited content.
- Suspension and removal.
- Verification and featured-placement disclosures.
- Intellectual-property permissions.
- Liability limits that are appropriate and enforceable.
- Governing law and dispute process.

## Recommended review point

Complete professional legal review after the commercial model and technical data flows are known, but before applications, payments, user accounts or sensitive-data collection are opened to the public.
