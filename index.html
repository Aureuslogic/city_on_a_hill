Our Purpose

Your Business Is More Than a Business

We believe enterprise can be an expression of calling, service and faithful stewardship. Christian entrepreneurs should be equipped to build businesses that solve real problems, serve people brilliantly, and create lasting value.

City on a Hill, powered by Dominion Mandate, brings marketplace visibility and business development together. We help Christian-owned businesses become easier to discover, better equipped to grow, and increasingly ready for larger opportunities.

For customers and organisations, we provide a trusted way to discover brilliant businesses offering products, professional services and transformational solutions.

“You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden.”
— Matthew 5:14

Our Commitment

Building Businesses That Serve a Greater Purpose

We Serve You

We begin with your vision, your calling and the people you exist to serve. City on a Hill gives you a place to be discovered, build meaningful relationships and access opportunities for growth.

We Help You Scale

Growth happens through Dominion Mandate. City on a Hill connects you to Dominion Mandate's coaching, Fulfilling Your Mandate programme, AI capability and practical business development — the tools to strengthen and scale what God has placed in your hands.

We Advance the Kingdom

When Kingdom businesses grow, they create employment, solve problems, generate wealth, transform communities, and increase their capacity to support the work of the Kingdom.

We Give Glory to God

Our ultimate ambition is not visibility for its own sake. It's to build businesses marked by excellence, integrity, generosity and faithful stewardship — so God is glorified through how we work, lead and serve.
